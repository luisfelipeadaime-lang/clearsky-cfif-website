About CFIF

What is CFIF?

CFIF is an industry-led Emissions Reduction/Fuel Production (ERFP) fund created specifically for primary fuel suppliers in Canada. It provides a simple, compliant pathway for fuel suppliers to meet their obligations under the Clean Fuel Regulations (CFR).

Instead of navigating complex project development or bilateral credit markets individually, participating fuel suppliers pool contributions into a professionally managed fund. CFIF then invests in a diversified portfolio of emissions-reduction and clean fuel projects across Canada.

The result is predictable compliance costs, reduced administrative burden, and meaningful contribution to Canada’s clean energy transition. CFIF is governed by the industry it serves, ensuring alignment between regulatory obligations and investment decisions.

Under the CFR, primary fuel suppliers may satisfy up to 10% of their annual reduction requirement through contributions to an approved ERFP fund. This cap is set by regulation and applies to all ERFP funds.

Bankability

CFIF Makes Projects Bankable. No Other ERFP Does This.

The biggest barrier to building Canadian clean fuel projects is not a lack of grant capital. It is that banks will not lend.

Regulatory uncertainty — stroke of pen risk — stops institutional lenders from participating in clean fuel project finance. If the CFR changes, project revenues could be stranded overnight. Banks see this and walk away. Otherwise sound, commercially viable projects die in development — not because of poor economics, but because no lender will step in.

CFIF is the only ERFP structuring its capital to absorb this risk — directly unlocking institutional project finance alongside its own capital. Compliance dollars become the catalyst for a far larger pool of private investment.

CFIF is actively exploring this approach in collaboration with major Canadian financial institutions.
