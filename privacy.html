Privacy Policy

How CFIF collects, uses, and protects your information.

Information We Collect

When you submit a form on this website (via our Contribute or Apply pages), we collect the personal information you provide, including your name, email address, company name, phone number, and any details included in your message or project description.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely to respond to your inquiry, evaluate potential contributions or project applications, and communicate with you about CFIF's fund activities. We do not use your information for marketing purposes unrelated to your inquiry.

Information Sharing

CFIF does not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information with CFIF's leadership team and professional advisors as necessary to evaluate your inquiry or application. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at CFIF@cleanfuels.ca.

This privacy policy is provided as a general framework. CFIF recommends that visitors consult their own legal advisors regarding data privacy matters. This policy may be updated from time to time.
