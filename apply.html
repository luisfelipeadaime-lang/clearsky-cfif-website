For Project Developers

Catalytic capital for Canadian clean fuel projects.

CFIF deploys 100% of contributed funds as non-repayable grants to eligible Canadian clean fuel and emissions-reduction projects. We also structure capital to make projects bankable to institutional lenders.

Submit Your Project Fund Mechanics

What CFIF Funds

Projects that reduce carbon intensity. At scale.

CFIF deploys capital to Canadian projects with clear pathways to verified emissions reductions and clean fuel production capacity.

Advanced biofuels

Ethanol, biodiesel, hydrogenated renewable diesel (HRD), and renewable diesel production facilities.

Renewable natural gas (RNG)

Anaerobic digestion, landfill gas capture, and biomethane upgrading projects.

Hydrogen production

Green and blue hydrogen projects with clear CI pathways under the CFR methodology.

Co-processing

Bio-based feedstocks co-processed in conventional petroleum refineries to reduce lifecycle carbon intensity.

Facility-level emissions reduction

Process efficiency improvements, methane capture, and CCUS at existing fuel production facilities.

Enabling infrastructure

Blending terminals, renewable fuel distribution, hydrogen refueling infrastructure, and EV charging infrastructure.

Grant structure

All CFIF funding is deployed as non-repayable grants. CFIF does not take equity or seek financial returns. Projects are not required to repay grant capital.

CFIF makes your project bankable

CFIF is the only ERFP structuring capital to absorb the regulatory risk that prevents banks from lending to clean fuel projects. A CFIF-backed project can attract institutional debt financing that would otherwise be unavailable.

Evaluation Process

What a strong application includes

Demonstrated emissions reductions

Projects must demonstrate a credible, measurable pathway to verified emissions reductions. CFIF evaluates the scale, permanence, and additionality of anticipated reductions.

Credible project proponent

Prior execution experience in the relevant fuel type or technology. CFIF evaluates management capability alongside project economics.

Defined project stage

Concept through operational and expansion-stage projects are all welcome. Early engagement helps CFIF understand the pipeline.

Commercial viability at scale

Projects must demonstrate a path to commercial viability. CFIF evaluates the economics of the project, not just the grant request.

Technology readiness

CFIF funds projects at Technology Readiness Level (TRL) 7 or above — technologies that have been demonstrated in an operational environment. Projects at TRL 9 receive higher priority in the evaluation process, as commercial-ready projects can deploy capital and generate verified emissions reductions more quickly.
