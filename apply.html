For Project Developers

Apply for non-repayable grant funding for eligible Canadian clean fuel projects.

What CFIF Funds

CFIF deploys 100% of contributions as non-repayable grants to Canadian projects with clear pathways to verified emissions reductions and clean fuel production capacity. Projects are evaluated on commercial and technical merit — the way a lender would assess them, not the way a grant program would. Where appropriate, CFIF structures capital to directly unlock institutional project finance alongside grant support — making CFIF-backed projects materially more bankable than those funded through other ERFP mechanisms.

Grant Structure

All CFIF funding is deployed as non-repayable grants. CFIF does not take equity or seek financial returns. Initial review takes 10 business days. Projects advancing to due diligence are notified directly.

What a Strong Application Includes

  • Clear CI reduction pathway with estimated gCO2e/MJ reduction
  • Credible project proponent with prior execution experience
  • Defined project stage (concept through operational/expansion all welcome)
  • Any existing funding secured or in-progress (public or private)
  • Commercial viability at scale
Eligibility

Who Can Apply

CFIF deploys 100% of contributed funds as non-repayable grants to eligible clean fuel and emissions-reduction projects across Canada. We welcome applications from project developers at any stage, from early concept to construction-ready.

Project Eligibility Criteria

  • Eligible under Canada's Clean Fuel Regulations
  • Located in Canada
  • Demonstrated emissions-reduction or clean fuel production potential
  • Credible project proponent with execution track record
  • Clear pathway to compliance credit generation
  • Third-party verifiable outcomes

Project Types

Low-Carbon Fuel Production

Renewable diesel, SAF, RNG, hydrogen, ethanol, biodiesel, and co-processed fuels.

Emissions Reduction

Process efficiency, methane capture, carbon capture, and fuel production decarbonization.

Enabling Infrastructure

Blending terminals, distribution networks, EV charging, and hydrogen fueling stations.
